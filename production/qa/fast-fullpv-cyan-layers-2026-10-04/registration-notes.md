# Cyan Registration and Static Evidence

Scope: actual source frames `[911,1008)`, 97 native frames. This work does not select artwork, change exposure mappings, render the production video, or grant artistic acceptance.

The actual source was decoded sequentially from `source_meta.json` through frame1284. All97 cyan frames are saved in `actual-source/`, with per-frame decoded pixel and PNG hashes in `source-decode.json`. All13 native800x120 caption pages were viewed. The long phrase runs through actual965; actual966 already shows the short phrase. The change occurs within canonical965's two-frame exposure, not at the next canonical967. See `source965-source966-native-caption-boundary.png` and page07.

`cyan_profile_layers.py` is gated by both the exact selected/expected asset filename and that cel's actual exposure interval. In particular975 uses `fast-fullpv-cyan-f0975-hair-v2.png` only at975..979 and981 uses `fast-fullpv-cyan-f0981-nod-v2.png` only at981..983; their rejected v1 files are not registered. Source and generated silhouettes are computed independently; existing approved original rigid matrices transform the generated matte together with its artwork. Source cropped long hair cannot seed exterior sky at the bottom canvas edge. Dark detached source outline strokes remain source anatomy. Vivid bright-blue enclosed hair gaps are excluded from opaque cels. Vacated original support uses the unchanged night helper's fit of visible current-source sky, plus local observed residuals. The hidden gradient is inferred reconstruction; it is neither a clean observed hidden plate nor generated sky. No rectangular clothing clear or global pale threshold was added.

`cyan_caption_layers.py` registers two phases: `[911,966)` with native region `(628,954,1264,1047)` and `[966,1008)` with region `(846,954,1048,1047)`. Long donors are actual911/914/919 source references. Short donors are actual967/973/981/993 references. Fixed observed cream color and moving-body donor votes identify the glyph support, including compression shifts where B>=G. Observable current glyph-core RGB is retained. Hidden plate and projected antialias alpha are reconstruction. Any1px contrast ring is constrained to actual new anatomy under registered glyph support and low contrast.

`compose.py` has only the named cyan branch, cyan caption restore call, and cyan pale-foreground exclusion. Existing dance, night-transition, raised-arms, and title helper files are untouched. `frozen-before-hashes.json` records all74 actual frozen outputs: `[897,911)` and `[1225,1285)`. The first after-change audit matched all74 decoded output pixel hashes exactly. A final hash audit follows the completed cyan rules.

The initial reused night sky classifier was rejected after actual composites retained old lavender fill; a temporary overly sparse sky sampling policy was also rejected because it left a visible inferred background boundary. Neither is the final helper state. The source-specific bottom-border flood correction resolved the cause.

Nine preliminary native composites were viewed:911/938/954/963/965/966/967/969/1006. The checked old long hair, animal-hat and neck strokes are removed and the generated outline is complete. Both caption phases are readable in the native crops. All observable glyph cores and all pixels outside registered old/new subject plus caption support were exact in this preliminary audit. This is bounded static and technical evidence, not original-speed artistic review. Once all15 new root assets are ready, the97 raw crops and final encoded crops are audited once as whole batches.
